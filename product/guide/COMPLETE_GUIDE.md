## Step 1: Set Up Your Channel (Day 1, Free)

### Create the Channel
1. Go to YouTube.com → Sign in or create account
2. Click your profile → "Create a channel"
3. Choose a name in these proven formats:
   - "[Adjective] [History/Archives/Files]" → "Iron Archives," "Forgotten Conflicts," "Steel History"
   - "[Topic] + [Dramatic Word]" → "Fallen Empires," "Lost Legions," "Buried Wars"
4. Write your About section (copy this template):
   > "Deep dives into history's most dramatic battles, forgotten empires, and the moments that changed the world. New video every week."
5. Upload a simple banner — black background, channel name in gold/silver serif font (use Canva free tier)

### Channel Settings
- Enable monetization application as soon as you start uploading (you need 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours)
- Set default language to English
- Set content rating to appropriate for all ages
- Category: Education

## Step 2: Create Your Videos (Days 2–3, Free)

### The Production Stack (All Free)
Every tool you need, costs zero dollars:

**Voiceover:** ElevenLabs (free tier: 10,000 characters/month)  
- Voice recommendation: "Adam" or "Antoni" — deep, authoritative, works perfectly for history narration
- Paid option ($5/month) gives unlimited characters — upgrade only after first revenue

**Video Assembly:** CapCut (free desktop version) or DaVinci Resolve (free)

**Stock Footage:** Pexels.com, Pixabay.com, Videvo.net — all free, all royalty-free  
- Search terms: "ancient army," "medieval battle," "ocean waves," "fire," "ruins," "forest fog"
- The footage doesn't have to perfectly match — atmospheric/evocative footage works well

**AI Art (for thumbnails and specific shots):** 
- Microsoft Designer (free, uses DALL-E)
- Adobe Firefly (free tier)
- Canva AI (free tier)
- Prompt template: "[historical scene], dramatic lighting, cinematic, dark atmosphere, 4k, ultra-realistic"

**Music:** Pixabay Music (free), YouTube Audio Library (free)  
- Search: "epic orchestral," "dramatic history," "dark ambient"
- Download 3-5 tracks per video — vary between dramatic and quiet/tense

**Thumbnail Design:** Canva (free tier)  
- Template: Dark background + dramatic image + bold white/yellow text
- Keep text to 3-5 words maximum
- Use high contrast — red, gold, or white on black always works

## Step 3: The Video Production Process

### For Each Script, The Process Takes 2–3 Hours:

**Hour 1: Generate Voiceover**
1. Open ElevenLabs → Text to Speech
2. Paste the script section by section (ElevenLabs free tier: paste in ~2,000 character chunks)
3. Export each section as MP3
4. Use Audacity (free) to combine sections and normalize audio levels

**Hour 2: Assemble Video**
1. Open CapCut or DaVinci Resolve
2. Add your audio track
3. Add stock footage clips — change every 3-6 seconds to maintain pace
4. Add music track at 15-20% volume under voiceover
5. Add text overlays for key names/dates (helps retention — viewers can read AND hear)

**Hour 3: Thumbnail + Upload**
1. Take the best dramatic frame from your video OR generate AI art
2. Open Canva → create 1280x720 thumbnail
3. Add bold text, adjust contrast/brightness
4. Export, upload to YouTube

### Title Formula (Critical for Algorithm)
Use this structure: **[Number/Superlative] + [Subject] + [Dramatic Result]**

Examples from scripts in this kit:
- "The 4 Minutes That Won World War II" ✓
- "How 20,000 Romans Walked Into a Trap" ✓
- "18,000 Winged Hussars vs 150,000 Ottomans" ✓

**Why it works:** Numbers create specificity (algorithm-friendly), the dramatic result creates curiosity. Both are required.

## Step 4: Posting Strategy

### First 30 Days: The Foundation
- Upload 1 video per week — consistency matters more than frequency at first
- Post on Friday afternoon (US time) — highest weekend watch time
- First 5 videos: use all 5 fully-written scripts from this kit (Scripts 01-05)

### Thumbnail A/B Testing (Free)
After 1,000 views, YouTube Studio lets you A/B test thumbnails. Always test:
- Version A: Face/character close-up + text
- Version B: Scene/battle + text
- Keep whichever gets higher CTR (click-through rate) after 7 days

### Comments Strategy
- Reply to every comment in the first 24 hours — YouTube rewards engagement velocity
- Pin a comment with your next video topic to build anticipation
- Ask a question at the end of every video: "Which battle should I cover next?"

## Step 6: Growth Hacks That Actually Work

### Reddit Distribution (Free, High Impact)
Post your video (or a written summary of it) to:
- r/history (4.5M members) — post as a text post summarizing the battle, link to video in comments
- r/AskHistorians — engage with questions, never spam links
- r/worldhistory — more lenient about video links
- r/badhistory — if your content corrects myths, this subreddit will love it

**Rule:** Never just post a link. Post a paragraph of interesting content, then say "Full video in comments" or "I made a documentary about this."

### Pinterest (Underrated for History Channels)
- Create pins for each video thumbnail + a brief description
- History content performs extremely well on Pinterest — the audience skews toward educated adults
- Use: Canva → export thumbnail → upload to Pinterest with title + link

### Cross-Promotion (Free)
Find 3-5 small history channels (1,000-10,000 subs) in complementary niches (military history, ancient history, world history). Comment genuinely on their videos. DM them about cross-promotion after a month of engagement. "Shoutout for shoutout" is a standard practice.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to show my face?**  
No. Every channel in this kit's data is faceless. Voice + footage + narration is the entire format.

**Q: What if my accent isn't American/British?**  
Use ElevenLabs — it will narrate with a neutral English accent. Nobody sees you.

**Q: How long until I make money?**  
YouTube Partner Program typically takes 2-4 months with weekly posting. Your first check will be small ($50-200). Month 4-6 is where revenue typically accelerates.

**Q: Can I change the scripts?**  
Yes — you own them. Add your own style, cut sections, add new research. The scripts are a foundation, not a script to read verbatim.

**Q: What if a video flops?**  
Expect 2-3 videos to underperform before you find your audience. The algorithm tests every video with a small initial audience — if CTR and watch time are good, it shows to more people. Don't delete underperforming videos. Just keep posting.

**Q: Do I need any equipment?**  
No. A laptop or desktop with internet access is everything you need.
